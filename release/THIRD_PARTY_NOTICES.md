# Third-party components and scientific assets

Solar System Sim uses SDL 3 under the zlib license. Its license is included in
`licenses/SDL3.txt`. OpenGL is provided by the user's graphics driver.

The scientific images, elevations and model assets have distinct providers and
credits. The source URLs, transformations, checksums and limitations are listed
in `assets/README.md` and the corresponding files under `docs/`:

- NASA / JPL / USGS: Mars MOLA, Mercury MDIS, Moon LOLA/LROC, planetary images,
  atmospheric visualization references, and Voyager/Galileo satellite mosaics.
- NASA Earth Observatory: Blue Marble; NASA SVS: GEOS-5 cloud visualization.
- NOAA NCEI: ETOPO 2022 relief, DOI 10.25921/fd45-gt74.
- NASA VTAD: planetary and satellite visualization models; individual credits
  and source pins appear in the body-specific documentation.

These credits describe provenance; they do not imply endorsement by the source
institutions. Display colors and animated weather are illustrative where noted.
This notice does not assign a license to the application's own source code.
